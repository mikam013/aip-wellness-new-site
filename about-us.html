A Note From Anya

Why I Started AIP Wellness

AIP Wellness didn't start as a coaching business — it started as a Pilates studio, launched right in the thick of my own perimenopause. I was already deep into healing myself, a journey that began in my gut and eventually pointed straight at my hormones. Somewhere along the way, teaching class after class, I noticed my clients were carrying the exact same weight I was.

The fatigue that wouldn't lift. The mood swings that came out of nowhere. Bloating with no obvious cause. That quiet, frustrating feeling of not being believed.

That's what pushed me to get certified — as a Gut Health Nutritionist, a Hormone Coach, and a Pilates Instructor — because these women (and I) deserved real answers, not more guesswork.

If there's one thing I want you to walk away with, it's this: you are not crazy. Midlife hits hard and hormones are no joke, but you're more resilient than it feels like right now — and balance is absolutely still within reach. Your body can heal, even now, even later than you think.

That conviction runs through everything I do. I'm not interested in magic pills or extreme diets — I care about sustainable change that actually fits your life. Wellness should feel doable, not like one more overwhelming thing on your plate. That's why I show up for the anxiety, the fatigue, the food struggles, the aches nobody can explain — and help you work through them by healing your gut and rebalancing your hormones, together.

I know firsthand how isolating this can feel when you're figuring it out alone. For years now, I've helped other women find the same strength and balance I had to fight for. I'd love to get to know the strong version of you that's been buried under all of this for too long. Let's bring her back.

xo, Anya
