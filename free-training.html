I've Been Where You Are

In my late 30s, I started feeling extremely anxious — worrying constantly over small things, carrying a sense of impending doom, replaying past decisions in my mind. At the same time, I felt physically exhausted. Even trying to get healthier felt overwhelming: should I eat this or that? Take this vitamin or that one? Through natural medicine and nutrition, I discovered that identifying where your hormones are out of balance can help you find the real you again — by nourishing the weak spots. I found my way back: I work out four days a week, eat a huge variety of foods, run my own business, and have the energy to show up for the people I love. I could not have done that without nourishing myself from the inside out and balancing my hormones.

3 Secrets Most People Don't Know

Secret #1

It's not how much you have to do, or your level of stress, that keeps you exhausted. Your body is like a luxury car — it needs premium fuel to perform at its peak. What you're fueling with matters more than how busy you are.

Secret #2

Exercise can absolutely contribute to your energy — but only if you balance it with your cycle, instead of working against yourself. The wrong workout on the wrong day can leave you more drained, not less.

Secret #3

Even the strongest, most successful people need a supportive environment. You are strong — you need an environment that makes you even stronger, which often means detoxing your environment too.
