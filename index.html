The Gut–Hormone Connection

Why Your Gut and Hormones Are Connected

Your gut talks to your whole body

Ladies, your gut does so much more than digest food. When it's out of balance, it can show up as low moods, afternoon energy crashes, and skin that just won't settle — which is why supporting your gut is so often where we start.

Hormone shifts change digestion, too

As estrogen and progesterone rise and fall in perimenopause, many women notice new bloating, slower digestion, or cravings that seem to come out of nowhere. You're not imagining it — your hormones and your gut are in constant conversation.

Why “eating healthy” stops working the same way

The meals that kept you feeling great ten years ago may not fit the body you're in now. Midlife changes how you handle sugar, stress, and muscle-building, so your plate needs to change with you, too. That's exactly what we work on together.

Frequently Asked Questions

I've already tried diets and supplements. Can this still help?

You're in such good company — so many women come to me after trying diet after diet and a cabinet full of supplements. The difference here is that we don't start with a one-size-fits-all plan. We start with you: your symptoms, your body, and your real life.

What's the difference between the Gut Health and Hormone programs?

Your gut and hormones are connected, so both programs touch on each other — the difference is where we start. 12-Week Gut Health Coaching focuses on digestion, like bloating, food sensitivities, and brain fog, with weekly 1:1 sessions and gut-supportive meal plans. The 12-Week Midlife Hormone Reset is for women in perimenopause dealing with anxiety, fatigue, weight fluctuations, or irregular cycles, with 1:1 hormone coaching and a hormone-focused nutrition plan.

You can see them side by side in the program comparison.

Will I have to cut out a lot of foods?

Nope! No magic pills, no extreme diets. Together we build a way of eating that's personalized to your body and realistic for your life, one small, doable change at a time.

How soon will I notice a difference?

Everyone is different, so I won't promise you a timeline. Many women notice small changes within the first few weeks, and those small wins add up. We'll check in on your progress together along the way, so you can see what's shifting.

How does online coaching work?

It's simple! We meet 1:1 over Zoom, so you can join from home, your office, or wherever life finds you — coaching is available to women anywhere in the US. Sessions are scheduled around your life, not the other way around.
